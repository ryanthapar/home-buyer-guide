A new build usually costs more to buy and less to run. A resale home is the reverse. This part compares the real cost of each, explains what you pay and risk while a new condo is being finished, and lists the Ontario warranty that protects you. It is based on a pre-construction briefing, which uses mostly US data, so the ownership-cost figures are examples to replace with your own.
